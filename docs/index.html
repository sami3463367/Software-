Moss & Ember

a cozy farm in the valley — slice 0.1

▶ Play now (single file, ~14 MB) downloads and runs offline — double-click it, no installation, no PC needed

How to play

  • New Game on the title screen, then walk to a tilled plot and press E to plant.
  • Come back when the crop is ripe and press E again to harvest, then sell it to Pip at the stall.
  • Keyboard: WASD move · Shift run · drag / Q,R camera · E interact · 1/2/3 seed · M mute
  • Phone/tablet: left thumb walks, drag the right side to look, the round button interacts.

What's in this slice

  • 12 farm plots, 3 crops (wheat / tomato / pumpkin) with growth stages
  • Plant → grow → harvest → sell → buy seeds economy
  • Day/night cycle, sunset, stars, a chicken you can pet, Pip the neighbour
  • Progress saves in the browser, so Continue works next time
Engine: Godot 4.7.2 (WebAssembly). Built in an AI-agent sandbox; the single-file build lets you play without any server or hosting.
